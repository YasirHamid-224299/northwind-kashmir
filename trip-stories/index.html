FAMILY VACATION CASE STUDY

The Sharma Family's Winter Wonderland Journey

"Traveling to Gulmarg with my elderly parents (aged 72 & 69) and a 5-year-old child in mid-January seemed challenging. The main concerns were central heating and safe road transport during heavy snowfall. Northwind Kashmir arranged a spacious Innova Crysta equipped with snow chains, and booked us in centrally heated rooms at Pine Spring Resort."

"Our guide, Bashir Bhat, was incredibly patient. He arranged wheelchairs at the Srinagar airport and secured Gondola Phase 1 tickets in advance, saving us hours of standing in queues. We enjoyed snow sledging at Kongdoori, local hot tea, and a cozy houseboat stay on Nigeen Lake. It was a flawless experience for the entire family."

"Northwind Kashmir removed all the worry of traveling with seniors in freezing weather." — Suresh Sharma, Delhi

HONEYMOON CASE STUDY

Ananya & Rohit's Romantic Houseboat Honeymoon

"We wanted our honeymoon in Kashmir to be private, scenic, and slow-paced. Northwind Kashmir designed a 7-day bespoke tour. Our first night was spent on Nigeen Lake in a premium wood-carved luxury houseboat. When we arrived, the room was beautifully decorated with fresh flowers, and we went on a private sunset Shikara ride."

"In Pahalgam, we stayed in a luxury cabin overlooking the Lidder River. Our driver-guide, Altaf, knew the best spots to avoid crowds, allowing us to take beautiful photographs at Aru Valley and Shalimar Gardens. The highlight was having hot Kahwa tea on our houseboat balcony at sunrise. Absolute heaven."

"The attention to detail on the honeymoon decoration and private Shikara dining was perfect." — Ananya Sen, Mumbai

GROUP ADVENTURE CASE STUDY

A 12-Man Adventure across Sonmarg & Gurez

"Our team of 12 from Bangalore wanted an adventure tour in Kashmir. We wanted white-water rafting, hiking, and camping, but away from the standard crowded tourist trails. Yasir Hamid planned an offbeat route starting in Sonmarg for rafting on the Sindh River, followed by a road trip to Gurez Valley near the LOC."

"Saba Jan, our mountain guide, coordinated the border permits for Gurez Valley smoothly. We hiked to the base of Habba Khatoon peak, camped by the roaring Kishanganga River, and spent evenings singing songs around campfires. Northwind Kashmir handled all local taxi union coordination in Sonmarg and provided excellent trekking gear. Highly recommended for groups!"

"Excellent coordination of Gurez border permits and Sindh River rafting gear." — Vikram Nair, Bangalore
