Quick Guide

Gondola Ride Phases

I

Phase I: Gulmarg to Kongdoori

Takes you through scenic pine forests up to 10,050 ft (3,050 m). Journey takes about 9 minutes. Perfect for beginners and families.

II

Phase II: Kongdoori to Apharwat Peak

Ascends up to Apharwat peak at a high altitude of 13,780 ft (4,200 m). Features dramatic snow vistas and panoramic views of K2.

Important Instructions

  • Valid ID Proof: Booking names must exactly match your Aadhaar, Passport, or Election card.
  • High Altitude: Phase II reaches over 13,000 ft. Please travel with caution if you have history of breathing issues.
  • Weather Conditions: Rides are subject to sudden atmospheric changes or maintenance slots. Secure booking assistance ensures quick updates.
