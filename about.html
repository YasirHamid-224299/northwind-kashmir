How We Plan

A better trip starts with a better conversation.

We begin by understanding what you want from Kashmir: the destinations that interest you, the dates you have, the pace you prefer, and the kind of stay that feels right.

From there, we can recommend a ready package or shape a more personal route across Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, Sonmarg, and other destinations already covered on the site. Details are discussed directly so the plan can reflect your actual trip rather than a generic checklist.
