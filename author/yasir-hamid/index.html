About Yasir Hamid

Born and raised in Tangmarg, the gateway to Gulmarg, Yasir Hamid grew up surrounded by the towering ridges of the Pir Panjal mountains. His passion for the local landscape led him to begin guiding hikes for tourists at an early age. Today, he is a respected local voice in J&K travel planning and tourism sustainability.

Over his 12 years in the industry, Yasir has logged hundreds of alpine trekking runs, managed winter sports logistics for professional snow sport skiers in Gulmarg, and established direct commercial partnerships with the finest houseboats and luxury resorts in Srinagar and Pahalgam.

In 2022, he co-founded **Northwind Kashmir** alongside proprietor and owner **Ajaz Ahmad Mir** to provide travelers with direct local hospitality (Kashmiriyat), tax-compliant ticketing, and on-ground safety security, eliminating third-party agent commissions.
